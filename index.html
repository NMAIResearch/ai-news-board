Motive tier

How to read this board · motive key, neutrality notes, tier map and source mix
Motive key (who benefits if the claim is true)
Tier 1: Primary record / regulator / adversarial process (least incentive)
Tier 2: Research institute or academia (credibility-aligned)
Tier 3: Analyst house or trade press (sells reports, access or clicks)
Tier 4: Tool or data vendor (product benefits from the framing)
Tier 5: Party selling the thing the claim is about (own topic)
Colour is incentive distance, not trust. A tier-5 source can be entirely correct; the tier says only where an independent second source is worth the effort.
How to read this, and where it is not neutral
  • Tier is claim-relative and based on an observable fact (what an entity sells or is), not a truth or quality verdict.
  • Colour is incentive distance, not trust: a tier-5 source can be entirely correct. The tier only says where an independent second source is worth the effort.
  • One curator sets these tiers (assisted by an AI model), unlike aggregators that average several rater organisations. So every cell is published with its basis and is open to challenge.
  • The reality anchor only covers topics this portfolio addresses, so claims near that work get more scrutiny than others. Most items carry no anchor, which is honest.
Conflict of interest: a frontier-lab (Anthropic) model helped assign these tiers, including the tiers on Anthropic and its rivals. It is tiered in its own map and not exempt.
